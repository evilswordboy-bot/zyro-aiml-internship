Research Paper Abstract: Emerging Architectures in Deep Learning
Abstract:
Recent breakthroughs in deep neural architectures have significantly advanced sequence modeling and multimodal understanding.
In this survey, we categorize contemporary attention mechanisms, discuss memory-efficient parameterization techniques, and benchmark retrieval-augmented generation pipelines across standard biomedical and legal corpora.
Our empirical results demonstrate a 14% reduction in computational latency without degradation in task performance.
