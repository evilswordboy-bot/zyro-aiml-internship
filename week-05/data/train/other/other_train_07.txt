Technical Project Proposal: Automated Data Ingestion Pipeline
Author: Cloud Architecture Core Team | Version: 2.1 | Date: October 2026
Executive Summary:
This proposal outlines the high-throughput architecture for processing multi-format enterprise documents.
The solution leverages optical character recognition, NLP entity extraction, and stateful workflow control.
Key Milestones & Estimated Timelines:
Phase 1: Ingestion Gateway & SHA-256 Deduplication (Week 1-2)
Phase 2: Document Classification and Rule Engine (Week 3-4)
Phase 3: Human Review Queue & Audit Logging System (Week 5)
Resource Allocation & Budget Estimates:
Engineering Team: 3 Full-Time Engineers over 6 months.
Cloud Infrastructure Costs: Estimated at $1,200 monthly during deployment.
